## 1. Corporate Overview & History

**Blue Phoenix Deepwater Limited** (*trading as Blue Phoenix Marine*) is an international subsea engineering, underwater ship Inspection, Repair & Maintenance (IRM), offshore terminal support, and marine logistics company registered under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, with International and Administrative Headquarters in Potters Bar, Hertfordshire, United Kingdom.

As a leading provider of subsea and marine services across West Africa and Europe, Blue Phoenix Deepwater deploys full-service surface-supplied and saturation diving spreads, Remotely Operated Vehicles (ROVs), and dedicated support vessels (*DSV Phoenix 1*). Its capabilities span:
- Subsea marine engineering, construction, survey, and inspection of offshore pipelines, SPM/CALM buoys, and FPSO/FSO platforms.
- Class-approved afloat underwater ship repairs, hull cleaning, propeller polishing, cofferdam installations, and In-Water Surveys (UWILD).
- Marginal oilfield crude cargo evacuation via specialized shuttle tankers and double-hull barges.
- Commercial shipbroking, chartering, and Offshore Support Vessel (OSV) supply.

## 5. Complete Portfolio of Services (23 Specialized Services)

### 5.1 Underwater Services (13 Units)

1. **Hull Cleaning (`/services/hull-cleaning`)**
   - In-water hull cleaning using bespoke *Mini-Pamper* and twin/single brush-kart vehicles to remove marine fouling, restore anti-fouling/silicone coating performance, reduce fuel consumption, and expose hull plating prior to surveys.
2. **Welding Repairs (`/services/welding`)**
   - Class-approved underwater wet and dry habitat (cofferdam) welding executed by dual-certified commercial diver-welders qualified to **BS EN ISO 15614-12** and **AWS D3.6M**. Includes shell plating crack repairs, bilge keel/rudder/skeg welding, anode installation, and pre/post-weld NDT (MPI, UT, Eddy Current).
3. **Insert Plate Repairs (`/services/insert-plate-repairs`)**
   - Permanent Class-approved afloat replacement of damaged or corroded hull plating using custom external watertight cofferdams and single-sided welding procedures. Proven on ships and offshore FPSOs with inserts as large as `2.5m x 1.0m` installed afloat.
4. **Stern Seal Servicing (`/services/stern-seal-servicing`)**
   - Afloat underwater stern tube seal replacement and repair inside custom dry hyperbaric habitats in partnership with leading seal OEMs. Includes lip-seal bonding and vulcanization, weardown gauge readings, shaft alignment checks, and rope guard refurbishment.
5. **Inspection In Lieu of Dry Docking — UWILD (`/services/dry-docking-inspection`)**
   - Comprehensive In-Water Surveys approved by all IACS Classification Societies (ABS, DNV, Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, ClassNK) using real-time color CCTV, two-way voice communications with attending surveyors, rudder pintle clearance checks, and tailshaft weardown measurements.
6. **Propeller Cleaning & Polishing (`/services/propeller-cleaning-polishing`)**
   - Multi-stage underwater hydrodynamic propeller polishing using high-suction (`100 kg`) rotary tools and diamond/abrasive pads down to 360-grit, achieving a **0.5µ Ra surface roughness finish (Rubert Scale Grade A)** for measurable bunker fuel savings.
7. **Video & Visual Inspection (`/services/video-visual-inspection`)**
   - High-definition subsea CCTV, macro still photography with calibration scales, and annotated defect mapping for pre-purchase, on-hire/off-hire, and structural integrity audits.
8. **ROV Inspection & Recording (`/services/rov-inspection-recording`)**
   - Portable observation-class and light work-class Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) deployments equipped with multi-beam imaging sonar, cathodic protection (CP) probes, and ultrasonic thickness sensors for deepwater hulls, FPSO mooring chains, risers, and pipelines.
9. **In-Water Thruster Repair & Replacement (`/services/thruster-repair-replace`)**
   - Afloat tunnel and azimuth thruster overhaul, controllable pitch (CPP) blade replacement, dry tunnel blanking for gearbox/seal repairs, and complete underwater exchange of azimuth pods and demountable thrusters.
10. **Propeller Repair & Replacement (`/services/propeller-repair-replace`)**
    - Dynamic balancing calculations, underwater cold straightening of bent Ni-Al-Bronze blade tips, precision hydrodynamic blade cropping, crack propagation arrest via NDT/fairing, and complete CPP blade swaps afloat.
11. **IACS Specialist Periodic Surveys (`/services/iacs-specialist-surveys`)**
    - Accredited intermediate, special, and damage in-water surveys executed by **CSWIP 3.1u / 3.2u** inspection divers with Ultrasonic Thickness (UT) gauging, MPI, ACFM, and CP potential readings.
12. **Painting & Adhesives Application (`/services/painting-adhesives`)**
    - Subsea surface preparation and application of hydrophobic underwater-curing epoxies, anti-corrosion barriers, splash-zone pile encapsulation, and fast-curing emergency leak-sealing composites.
13. **Voith Schneider Propeller (VSP) Repair & Replace (`/services/voith-schneider-propeller`)**
    - Specialist underwater extraction and replacement of vertical cycloidal VSP blades, lower rotor casing seal renewal, and guard plate reinforcement on tractor tugs and OSVs.
